The boards of the table
=======================

The boards in this folder are the boards of lichess, which are real
boards: wood with a grain, marble, leather, plastic, canvas, and the
rest. They are copied with the game, so a table looks the same on every
device and with no internet.

    https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/images/board

Every one of them is a whole board, the squares and all, so the board is
drawn as one picture and the pieces are put on top of it.

The licence
-----------

The boards are the work of the lichess authors and of pirouetti, and they
are released under the AGPLv3+:

    https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/COPYING.md

The colours of the coordinates on each board are the ones lichess uses, so
the letters on a board are as readable here as they are there. They are
in `src/lib/boardThemes.ts`.
