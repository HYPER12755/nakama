The pieces of the board
======================

The sets of pieces in this folder come from the lichess client, which keeps
them beside the games it shows:

    https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece

Each set is drawn by its own artist and is released under its own licence.
The list below names the artist and the licence of every set, so the pieces
can be traced back to the people who drew them.

The MoonGames pieces are not in this list. They are drawn by the game
itself, as the artwork in the piece component, and are the default.

A note about the non commercial sets
------------------------------------

The sets marked CC BY-NC-SA, and the alpha set, may not be used for a
commercial purpose. They are here because they are beautiful, and they are
kept apart from the rest so the choice is easy to make. A table that is
offered for money should use one of the sets that allows it: cburnett,
chessnut, celtic, fantasy, spatial, letter, merida, mpchess, pix...

Every set is a folder of twelve drawings, one for each piece of each
colour. The names are the same in every set, so the game can draw any of
them in the same way.
