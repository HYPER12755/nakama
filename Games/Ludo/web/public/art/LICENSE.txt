Copied visual art
=================

The dice textures and token art under public/dice/ and public/tokens-copied/
were extracted directly from the user-supplied Ludo King APK in ~/unzip/.
They are included as selectable cosmetic sets in this MoonGames build.

The added stylish token set came from the base scene assets:
  StylishToken_Red / Green / Yellow / Blue -> tokens-copied/stylish/token-1..4.png

No board art was copied: Ludo keeps the supplied matching board so the token
coordinates remain exact.
