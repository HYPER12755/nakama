Ludo sound credits
==================

Two selectable sound sets are bundled. Both are real recordings; neither is
synthesised.

1. Recorded table (public/sounds/ludo)
--------------------------------------

dice-roll.mp3 / dice-roll.ogg
  "Shake and Roll Dice" by Mike Koenig, Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0).
  Edited down from soundbible.com/182-Shake, distributed with tremby/dice:
  https://github.com/tremby/dice/blob/main/public/roll.mp3

move.ogg, capture.ogg
  wooden_01.ogg and wooden_02.ogg from lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds,
  released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).
  https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds

home.ogg, turn.ogg, start.ogg, win.ogg, foul.ogg
  Beep3, Beep7, Click1, Beep1 and Beep5 from the MMRetro Arcade Sounds Pack in
  lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds, released under CC0 1.0.
  https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds

2. Ludo King (APK) (public/sounds/ludo-apk)
-------------------------------------------

These clips were extracted directly from the user-supplied Ludo King APK at
~/unzip/com.ludo.king.apk, from assets/bin/Data/sharedassets0.resource.
The original recordings belong to the Ludo King game and its publisher; they
are included here because the user supplied the APK for this MoonGames build.

Original clip names used here:

  dice.ogg        <- dice (stereo dice roll; restored after review)
  dice2.mp3       <- dice2 (second real dice roll; restored after review)
  dice2.ogg       <- dice2 (same recording in OGG form, kept for comparison)
  move.ogg        <- cursor_move
  capture.ogg     <- coins_collect
  home.ogg        <- up_level_small
  foul.ogg        <- net_time (short cue; replaces the hissing down_level clip)
  turn.ogg        <- turn
  start.ogg       <- start_game
  win.ogg         <- up_level_big

The FSB5 banks were decoded on this device with python-fsb5 and vgmstream,
then converted to OGG/MP3 with FFmpeg.

Extraction tooling:
  python-fsb5   https://github.com/HearthSim/python-fsb5
  vgmstream     https://github.com/vgmstream/vgmstream

The attributions above must travel with the game bundle.
