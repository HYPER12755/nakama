Carrom table sounds
===================

These are real recordings of wooden carrom pieces, not generated tones. The
sounds were taken from a carrom game APK distributed on GitHub:

  https://github.com/EnnioMrk/Carrom-releases/releases/download/v0.74.7/dabbu-carrom-v0.74.7-debug.apk

and from the open Unity carrom project:

  https://github.com/sanjitsarkar/Carrom/tree/master/Assets/SFX

They are included here so the table can make the sound of a real striker hit,
a man dropping into a pocket, a queen being covered and a foul. If the rights
holder objects, the files can be replaced without changing the game: each
sound set is a folder of seven mp3 files named strike, pocket, queen, foul,
start, turn and win.
